Productivity 101

FOUNDATION

What is productivity?

Completed output divided by the resources used to produce it.

Output ÷ Input

Example: order lines per labor hour.

INBOUND

Inbound productivity

Inbound work completed relative to inbound labor hours.

900 lines ÷ 100 hours = 9 lines/hour
OUTBOUND

Outbound productivity

Outbound work completed relative to outbound labor hours.

1,200 lines ÷ 150 hours = 8 lines/hour
SERVICE

Productivity vs OTIF / OTD

OTIF measures service reliability. OTD measures order-to-delivery elapsed time. Neither is directly a labor productivity ratio.
